What the pictures come from

  • The real picture is a public domain photograph, pulled live from Wikimedia Commons for the subject of the round.
  • The fake is made on the spot by a Pollinations image model from a one line prompt of the same subject, so it is always fresh.
  • Three demo rounds are bundled with the page, so anybody can play before signing in.
  • Twelve seconds a round. The timer bar is the pressure, the tell after the reveal is the lesson.
  • Signing in is a plain OAuth + PKCE loop in your browser, and your own Pollen pays for the generated picture. Nothing is proxied.
