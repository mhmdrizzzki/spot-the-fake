# Spot the Fake

Two pictures of the same thing. One is a photograph, one was drawn by a machine. Pick the fake before twelve seconds are up.

- The real half is a public domain photograph pulled live from Wikimedia Commons for the subject of the round.
- The fake half is generated on the spot by a Pollinations image model (`flux`) from a one line prompt of the same subject.
- Sign in with Pollinations (OAuth + PKCE in the browser, nothing proxied). Your own Pollen pays for the generated picture.
- Twelve seconds a round, score and streak in the header, and a tell after every reveal so you learn what to look for.

Play it: https://mhmdrizzzki.github.io/spot-the-fake/
